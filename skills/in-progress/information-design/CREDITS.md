# Credits

The original view catalog (pseudocode, call trees, component trees, file trees, Mermaid, diffs) and its placement guidance come from [Dex Horthy](https://github.com/dexhorthy)'s [`show-me`](https://github.com/humanlayer/skills/blob/main/plugins/show-me/skills/show-me/SKILL.md) skill. It first landed in this repo copied into `pr`; it moved here so callers share one copy.

The expanded explanation method draws on these design references, expressed here as task-specific instructions rather than copied passages:

- [Amit Patel: Introduction to A*](https://www.redblobgames.com/pathfinding/a-star/introduction.html), for inspectable execution and coordinated comparisons.
- [Bartosz Ciechanowski: Mechanical Watch](https://ciechanow.ski/mechanical-watch/), for revealing mechanisms through recognizable parts.
- [Bret Victor: Up and Down the Ladder of Abstraction](https://worrydream.com/LadderOfAbstraction/), for connecting aggregate views to concrete instances.
- [Grant Sanderson: advice to explanation creators](https://www.3blue1brown.com/about/), for concrete examples before abstraction and purposeful animation.
- [Edward Tufte: Envisioning Information](https://www.edwardtufte.com/book/envisioning-information/), for small multiples, layering, and comparison.
- [Nicky Case: How I Make Explorable Explanations](https://blog.ncase.me/how-i-make-an-explorable-explanation/), for concrete experience and consequential teaching sequences.
- [Richard Mayer: principles for reducing extraneous processing](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/cambridge-handbook-of-multimedia-learning/principles-for-reducing-extraneous-processing-in-multimedia-learning-coherence-signaling-redundancy-spatial-contiguity-and-temporal-contiguity-principles/C98AB3A6CE760DD63C048936EA0B3B44), for coherence, signaling, and spatial contiguity.
