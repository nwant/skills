# Credits

Adapted from the `verification-before-completion` skill in [superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), rewritten in this repo's voice. The gate, the table of claims against the evidence each needs, and the rule that a subagent's report is not evidence come from there. The **Provenance** section draws on problems reported against that skill in its issue tracker.
